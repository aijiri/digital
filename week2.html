B. Workshop an AI Tool

  1. Open Google Gemini with your KUFS account.
  2. Answer prompts a-d below. Use as much detail as you can, following the examples above.
    1. What do you look like in this dream?
    2. Where are you located in this dream?
    3. What are you doing that represents this dream?
    4. What visual features do you want in this dream?
  3. Look at your image. If it's not quite right, revise your prompt and generate again.
  4. Once you have an image you like, share it in a post on our TEAMS channel.

Use Generative AI to create an image that doesn't exist.

Before you start, look at these examples so you know what level of detail to aim for.

Example: (a) What do you look like in this dream?

Weak: "A person in space."

Acceptable: "A woman in a silver spacesuit floating near Saturn's rings."

Highly detailed: "A woman in her 30s wearing a reflective silver spacesuit with a cracked helmet visor, floating weightlessly against a backdrop of Saturn's rings, lit by distant starlight, digital art style."

Example: (b) Where are you located in this dream?

Weak: "In a city."

Acceptable: "On a rooftop in a futuristic city at night."

Highly detailed: "On a glass rooftop terrace overlooking a neon-lit futuristic city at night, flying cars streaking past in the distance, rain reflecting the lights below."

Example: (c) What are you doing that represents this dream?

Weak: "Working."

Acceptable: "Presenting an idea to a small team."

Highly detailed: "Standing confidently in front of a holographic display, presenting a glowing 3D model to three colleagues who look engaged and impressed."

Example: (d) What are some visual features that you would like to represent this dream?

Weak: "Cool colors."

Acceptable: "Blue and purple lighting with a futuristic feel."

Highly detailed: "Deep blue and violet lighting, soft glowing edges on every object, a subtle haze in the air, and sleek chrome surfaces throughout the scene."

Creating an effective AI image is a process, not a one-shot task. Expect to revise your prompt two or three times before the image matches what you imagined. If the first result feels off, look at what's missing or wrong, then add or change details and try again.
