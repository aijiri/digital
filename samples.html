This page will be updated with real student submissions from the TEAMS channel as they come in. For now, here is a model example showing the level of detail to aim for when answering the four dream prompts.

Model Example: "Future You" Dream

(a) What do you look like in this dream?
A tall Japanese boy with short black hair, wearing a futuristic pilot's uniform with holographic displays on the sleeves.

(b) Where are you located in this dream?
A floating city in the clouds with sleek, white architecture and sky bridges connecting various floating platforms.

(c) What are you doing that represents this dream?
Piloting a small aircraft between platforms, checking the holographic controls on his sleeve as he navigates toward a docking bay.

(d) What are some visual features that you would like to represent this dream?
The city is filled with lush green parks, transparent domes housing botanical gardens, and advanced transportation like hover cars and magnetic trains, with observation decks overlooking the clouds below. Rendered in a clean, cinematic, high-detail style.

Why this works: each answer is specific enough that Gemini has real details to build an image from, not just a general idea. Notice how (c) and (d) add small extra details (checking controls, docking bay, green parks, hover cars) that make the scene feel complete.

B1-Level Example: Weak, Acceptable, Highly Detailed

Here is the same idea written at three levels of detail, using simple B1-friendly English.

Weak:
"A girl with cats in space."

Acceptable:
"A girl in a kimono sitting with two white cats. Behind her, you can see space with the Earth and moon."

Highly Detailed:
"A girl is sitting outside. She is wearing a beautiful kimono with flowers on it. She has cat ears on her head, like a costume. Two white cats are sitting next to her. Behind her, you can see space. There is a big circle showing Earth, the moon, and stars. The picture has soft colors: pink, orange, blue, and grey. The style looks like a gentle, hand-painted drawing."

Why this works: the highly detailed version answers what, who, where, and what it looks like, using short sentences and everyday words instead of complex grammar. That is exactly the kind of detail Gemini needs to build a strong image.
