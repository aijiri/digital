This page will be updated with real student submissions from the TEAMS channel as they come in. For now, here is a model example showing the level of detail to aim for when answering the four dream prompts.

Example: Weak, Acceptable, Highly Detailed

Here is the same idea written at three levels of detail, using simple B1-friendly English.

Weak:
"A girl with cats in space."

Acceptable:
"A girl in a kimono sitting with two white cats. Behind her, you can see space with the Earth and moon."

Highly Detailed:
"A girl is sitting outside. She is wearing a beautiful kimono with flowers on it. She has cat ears on her head, like a costume. Two white cats are sitting next to her. Behind her, you can see space. There is a big circle showing Earth, the moon, and stars. The picture has soft colors: pink, orange, blue, and grey. The style looks like a gentle, hand-painted drawing."

Why this works: the highly detailed version answers what, who, where, and what it looks like, using short sentences and everyday words instead of complex grammar. That is exactly the kind of detail Gemini needs to build a strong image.
