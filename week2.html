B.Workshop an AI Tool

Use Generative AI to create an image that doesn't exist.

  1. Open Google Gemini with your KUFS account.
  2. Answer prompts a-d below. Use as much detail as you can, following the examples above.
    1. What do you look like in this dream?
    2. Where are you located in this dream?
    3. What are you doing that represents this dream?
    4. What visual features do you want in this dream?
  3. Look at your image. If it's not quite right, revise your prompt and generate again.
  4. Once you have an image you like, share it in a post on our TEAMS channel.

Creating an effective AI image is a process, not a one-shot task. Expect to revise your prompt two or three times before the image matches what you imagined. If the first result feels off, look at what's missing or wrong, then add or change details and try again.

Need more support? Check the glossary for key terms, the tips on prompting page for strategies, or sample student work to see what past students created.
